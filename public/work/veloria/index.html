THE PROBLEM

A real starting point.

A collection of creative ideas needs enough structure to belong together, while leaving each idea room to develop its own voice.

THE APPROACH

Make the relationships clear.

VELORIA uses an endorsed brand architecture. The parent identity connects six divisions, while The Daily Gaze, Clouds, Aura, The Grid, Roots and The Collection address different kinds of creative work.
