BANUJA DISANAYAKA / THE LONGER STORY

Curiosity is

PEOPLE / PROCESS

The operator

Understand the real work.

Working in an outlet means paying attention to people, timing and the details that hold a day together. Team coordination, stock, suppliers, production and order handling gave me a practical view of how small gaps between tasks become larger problems.

That experience feeds directly into TFC Orders Tracker: organise the handoff, make the next action clear and keep exceptions visible.

IDEAS / SYSTEMS

The builder

Turn a question into a tool.

Building products gives me a way to work on the problems I notice. My projects explore operational visibility, student administration, local knowledge and voice assistance. Each asks a different question about how software can support everyday work.

HOW THE DISCIPLINES CAME TOGETHER

A journey through
making and doing.

From Colombo into creative work

My school years were at Thurston College, Colombo 07, from 2011 to 2021, through G.C.E. O/L. I then moved into graphic design studies at NSBM and software engineering foundation studies through Curtin. Studying visual communication and technical foundations gave me two different ways to approach a problem.

My early professional work included online logo design with Multitasker and graphic design at WFI Education in Sri Lanka. Those roles brought design into practical contexts: a mark needs to be recognisable, and a message needs to reach the person it is intended for.

Learning through work in Malaysia

In Malaysia, my experience included project support with Sunny Aircon and a Senior Graphic Designer role at Hua Seng Advertising. Alongside that wider journey, I studied graphic design at YES College during 2023–2026.

At The Food Company in Kuala Lumpur, I worked as an Outlet PIC / Operations Executive from September 2024 to July 2026. Team coordination, POS closing and reconciliation, stock and supplier coordination, production support and multi-channel order handling brought me close to the complexity of running an operation.

Bringing that experience into products

Operations gave my product work a practical starting point. TFC Orders Tracker explores coordination between orders, production and fulfilment. Class Tracker TDG brings a similar concern for clear records and dependable corrections into tuition administration.

Second Brain and Banu 2.0 explore a different side of that curiosity: ownership of personal knowledge and the relationship between a voice conversation and an action. These projects are at different stages, and their case studies explain what exists, what shaped the architecture and what still needs validation.

Keeping a place for visual curiosity

The Daily Gaze is where observation becomes photography. The archive brings together ordinary moments, city scenes, nature and heritage from Sri Lanka and Malaysia. It is part of my practice in its own right, as well as a reminder to look carefully before making a decision.

VELORIA connects another part of my creative work: a shared framework for photography, stories, learning and digital ideas. My role brings together social media strategy, design, production and operations within a collaborative structure led by Akashna as CEO.

HOW I APPROACH THE WORK

Curiosity, with
a practical direction.

01

Start with the situation

Before choosing the interface or visual treatment, I want to understand the job: who is doing it, what they need, and where things become unclear. Real constraints give the work a useful direction.

02

Make the relationships visible

A workflow, a page hierarchy and a brand family all depend on relationships. I look for a structure that helps someone understand what belongs together and what should happen next.

03

Build, then examine it

A working version is a way to test an idea. Source review, practical checks and feedback matter alongside appearance. AI-assisted tools can help the process, but a generated result still needs judgement and verification.

04

Explain the current state honestly

A prototype, a working application and a finished release are different stages. I want the portfolio to show the thinking and the evidence at each stage, including the work still to be done.
